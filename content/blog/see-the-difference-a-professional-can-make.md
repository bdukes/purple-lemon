<p>We all snap photos of our kids and, these days, even of ourselves.&nbsp; We snap photos of almost everywhere we go, each date we&rsquo;re on, and document even what we ate for lunch. Even though I was late to the smartphone world, I&rsquo;m still guilty as charged.</p>
<p>And as I reviewed my cell phone pics to illustrate this post, realistically I have photos of that chair I cleaned really well. The cool pattern the syrup made in our yogurt when I was shaking the last drops out of the jar. A selfie to ask a friend if I can actually pull off this look (I wasn&rsquo;t exactly trendy- I was wearing a bonnet). And endless LEGO and magnet creations my kids couldn&rsquo;t bear to take apart without a picture.</p>
<p>My hubby, though technically a partner in Purple Lemon Photography, who even attends photography conferences like a champ, isn&rsquo;t a photographer. He collectively owns our cameras and uses the camera on his cell phone like many other people. Yet, he isn&rsquo;t a photographer. If I say to him, &ldquo;Pssst! Look at the boys, take a picture of that!&rdquo; he&rsquo;ll dutifully whip out the cell phone, take 14 frames in a row that all pretty much look the same and aren&rsquo;t at all what I imagined. Yes, the boys were still doing the cute thing that I wanted to see again, but the timing and composition were not quite up to par with my imagination.</p>
<p>For example, he thought it would be clever for our sons to become a mermaid, a unicorn, and a fairy for my birthday. A picture would certainly help us remember this whimsical moment of fantasy and good cheer. But&hellip; rather we have a cluster of blurry, confusing pictures of my sons in half of their PJs.</p>
<p><img src="https://res.cloudinary.com/purple-lemon-photography/image/upload/f_auto,fl_keep_attribution.lossy,q_auto/v1543345985/Posts/Files/Screenshot_2018-10-18_12.04.05__fzpnmf" alt="Series of images of children in handmade costumes" class="image__wide" width="1711" height="502" /></p>
<p>This is so easy to have happen! There is a time and place for new photographers to learn and grow, and I certainly needed and appreciated those friends and clients who provided that for me.&nbsp; However, there is now, more than ever a need for couples and families to find professional photographers who can help them have photos that are better than imagined, not less than.</p>
<p><img src="https://res.cloudinary.com/purple-lemon-photography/image/upload/f_auto,fl_keep_attribution.lossy,q_auto/v1543345986/Posts/Files/McLean_Family-014__t2wgip" alt="Portrait of young boy" class="image__wide" width="1000" height="664" /></p>
<p>A professional will weed out the 14 weird faces when you are half blinking or chewing your last bite of dinner roll.&nbsp; A professional will have a compelling composition and lighting that flatters, not distracts. A professional will deliver your product in a timely manner and treat you with respect. They will not require you to have all the ideas because that&rsquo;s their job. You get to keep being a teacher, a mom, a social worker, graphic designer, or- like my hubby- a software developer. I&rsquo;ll be the professional photographer so you don&rsquo;t have to be.</p>

<p>Although I have been photographing for many years, and photographing portraits since 2004, I completed the hard work to become a Certified Professional Photographer in 2012. This process requires passing a challenging exam on technical photography, passing a portfolio review including client work, and also a review of professional standards, websites, and a commitment to maintaining professional standards over time.&nbsp; To maintain this certification, I attend conferences, workshops, and classes each year to keep learning and keep my standards high. I also serve as a CPP Liaison who assists incoming candidates in completing their certification process.</p>
<p><img src="https://res.cloudinary.com/purple-lemon-photography/image/upload/f_auto,fl_keep_attribution.lossy,q_auto/v1543345987/Posts/Files/Raines_Wedding-033__x6rthi" alt="Close-up of a woman's white wedding dress" class="image__wide" width="1000" height="664" /></p>
<p>When considering your photographer, consider their qualifications in helping you celebrate your family, children, or wedding day. You can use Professional Photographers of America&rsquo;s database to help <a href="https://www.findaphotographer.com/">find a professional or Certified Professional Photographer</a>.</p>
