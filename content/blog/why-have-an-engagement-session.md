<p>Over a glass of lemonade, I tell each couple who sits with me in my sunny office the two reasons why I encourage couples to have an engagement session. Having portraits taken during this special season prior to marriage is both practical and a gift.</p>
<p><img class="image__wide" src="https://res.cloudinary.com/purple-lemon-photography/image/upload/f_auto,fl_lossy.keep_attribution,q_auto/v1553393375/Posts/Files/Rachel_Justin_Engagement-040__rrwmfm" alt="Exterior shot of The London Tea Room, through the window a couple is laughing" width="1000" height="664" /></p>
<p><img class="image__wide" src="https://res.cloudinary.com/purple-lemon-photography/image/upload/f_auto,fl_lossy.keep_attribution,q_auto/v1553358211/Posts/Files/Rachel_Justin_Engagement-002__twa3fu" alt="Painted heart with crown on a textured wall near Tower Grove Park" width="1000" height="664" /></p>
<p>First of all, it gives the couple an opportunity to get to know me with a camera and what that experience is like. It can be daunting to have someone following you all day with a camera on your wedding day. This gives us a chance to grow more comfortable together so you can better enjoy your wedding day. And on the flip side, I get to know you better and how you photograph. I learn things like if you blink a lot, or if you giggle with the attention. I can learn that you need a lot of direction or you naturally fall into poses. These things help your wedding day go more smoothly since we&rsquo;ve had a low-stress chance to practice together.</p>
<p><img class="image__wide" src="https://res.cloudinary.com/purple-lemon-photography/image/upload/f_auto,fl_lossy.keep_attribution,q_auto/v1553358296/Posts/Files/Rachel_Justin_Engagement-019__enc0tg" alt="Tea, cream, and biscuits at a tea shop in St. Louis" width="1000" height="664" /></p>
<p><img class="image__wide" src="https://res.cloudinary.com/purple-lemon-photography/image/upload/f_auto,fl_lossy.keep_attribution,q_auto/v1553358295/Posts/Files/Rachel_Justin_Engagement-010__qpfqjw" alt="Aerial view of a biscuit with jam and butter on a white plate" width="1000" height="664" /></p>
<p>Secondly, I get to photograph you in a special part of your life, with special details that you&rsquo;ll want to look back upon with fondness. I help you plan a date or location that holds meaning to you and helps tell your story. Some couples have recreated first dates or favorites. Some check things off their bucket list. And others aim for something more simple.</p>
<p><img class="image__wide" src="https://res.cloudinary.com/purple-lemon-photography/image/upload/f_auto,fl_lossy.keep_attribution,q_auto/v1553358295/Posts/Files/Rachel_Justin_Engagement-012__gxjngy" alt="A man, sitting at a table in a cafe, smiles at his fiancee" width="1000" height="664" /></p>
<p><img class="image__tall" src="https://res.cloudinary.com/purple-lemon-photography/image/upload/f_auto,fl_lossy.keep_attribution,q_auto/v1553394259/Posts/Files/Rachel_Justin_Engagement-034__zekqeu" alt="Well-dressed man smirks while his partner leans closely during a date" width="664" height="1000" /></p>
<p>I&rsquo;ve accompanied people on boat rides, watched people make brownies and throw popcorn, and taken others for hikes. I love the variety of the couples I photograph!</p>
<p><img class="image__wide" src="https://res.cloudinary.com/purple-lemon-photography/image/upload/f_auto,fl_lossy.keep_attribution,q_auto/v1553393376/Posts/Files/Rachel_Justin_Engagement-029__ru6gjo" alt="African American woman smiles at her fiancée" width="1000" height="664" /></p>
<p><img class="image__tall" src="https://res.cloudinary.com/purple-lemon-photography/image/upload/f_auto,fl_lossy.keep_attribution,q_auto/v1553394259/Posts/Files/Rachel_Justin_Engagement-050__i8jcg2" alt="Couple poses for indoor engagement portrait in Tower Grove Park area of South Saint Louis" width="664" height="1000" /></p>
<p>I love the chance to make sure that during this busy time of planning a big event and planning a future, often with finding a new apartment or house and sometimes a big move, you get at least one awesome date. You can look into each other&rsquo;s eyes, laugh, and hold hands. You get to connect over something that is uniquely you, and — BONUS! — then you get record of it for keeps.</p>
<p><img class="image__wide" src="https://res.cloudinary.com/purple-lemon-photography/image/upload/f_auto,fl_lossy.keep_attribution,q_auto/v1553358328/Posts/Files/Rachel_Justin_Engagement-016__phpuii" alt="Black couple conversing over tea and biscuits in front of a window" width="1000" height="664" /></p>
<p><img src="https://res.cloudinary.com/purple-lemon-photography/image/upload/f_auto,fl_lossy.keep_attribution,q_auto/v1553394355/Posts/Files/Rachel_Justin_Engagement-054__lvicfa" alt="Curls falling into the face of a young woman laughing" width="1000" height="664" class="image__wide" /></p>
<p>Recently, just a week before their wedding, when things could have felt busy and overwhelming, Justin and Rachel said &ldquo;Pause!&rdquo; and enjoyed breakfast together. Rachel had just moved back to town and the wedding was just about to happen, but I joined them at <a href="https://thelondontearoom.com/">The London Tea Room</a> where they enjoyed tea and breakfast. They said that cream was so good it was worth the splurge!</p>
