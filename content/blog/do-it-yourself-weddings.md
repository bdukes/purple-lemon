<p>As I&rsquo;m in my fourteenth year as a wedding photographer, it is amazing to look back and consider the amazing <abbr title="Do-it-Yourself">DIY</abbr> brides that I&rsquo;ve photographed over the years.&nbsp; We really cater to the DIY wedding couples who value photography, yet are looking to both express themselves and cut back on wedding expenses.</p>
<p>Here are my top handful of ways that you can showcase your own talents, personality, and family heirlooms on your wedding day without breaking the bank.</p>

<h3>DIY Guest books</h3>
<p>When I was a kid my parents stuck by the tradition of a guestbook on a stand by the front door.&nbsp; As guests stopped by, they&rsquo;d sign the book, and occasionally we&rsquo;d go back through and reminisce about the people who had filled our home. Even today, though that tradition is rare, the wedding guest book still remains. In many weddings though, the book itself is often replaced with more personal or decorative options.</p>
<p>Some of my clients have used rocks, thumbprints, or plates for guests to sign.</p>
<p>One favorite DIY guestbook was a vintage copy of a favorite novel.</p>
<p>Though not entirely DIY, we offer a photo guest book that showcases engagement photography.&nbsp; This is certainly personal and a keepsake for years to come.</p>

<p>I&rsquo;ve seen s&rsquo;mores, handmade pies and cakes, and even donuts!</p>

<h3>DIY Wedding Activities</h3>
<p>Dinner and dancing may be the best route for many, but we&rsquo;ve had many couples pass in front of my lens that were really seeking to find an alternative to those traditional activities- something to suit their guests and themselves better.</p>
<p>I&rsquo;ve attended bonfire receptions, seen handmade beanbag toss games, board game rooms, and even Guitar Hero competitions and costumes!</p>
<p>It&rsquo;s fun for your guests when you consider their preferences and are willing to think outside the box. As much as I love hitting the dance floor, I know just as many friends who would much rather grab a cup of hot cocoa and play a board game or do a puzzle at their table.</p>
