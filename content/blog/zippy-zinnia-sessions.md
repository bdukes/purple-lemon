<p><abbr title="Purple Lemon Photography">PLP</abbr> exists to be able to give back to compassionate organizations and ministries around the world. A few years ago, I began cutting used denim with friends to help make shoes for kids in Uganda who are suffering from jiggers, which is a parasite that can damage their feet. <a href="https://solehope.org/">Sole Hope</a> is doing amazing work to help heal these kiddos and their families. To ship the cut denim to the Ugandan shoemakers, each shoe needs to be sponsored. I've taken a break for a while, but for my birthday, I was given a new kit and I have loads and loads of denim in my basement that I'd love to cut and send.</p>
<p>A friend has graciously allowed the use of her garden to help us sponsor shoes. For the remainder of the growing season this year*, I'm offering 2 Sole Hope Specials.</p>
<p>1. Zippy Zinnia Sessions- $80, 10 min session with a bouquet, 2 digital files and the flowers to keep (Or all the digitals for $120)- $20 goes to Sole Hope</p>
<p>2. Or add a zinnia bouquet to any session for $20+ donation.&nbsp;</p>
<p>Great for anniversaries, engagements, mamas, daughters, sisters, friends!</p>
<p>Also, outside of pictures, you can get a small bouquet for $5 or a larger bouquet for $10. Send me a message to coordinate a pickup/delivery.</p>
<p><small>*As of August 18, the zinnia season is starting to wind down, but still available.</small></p>
