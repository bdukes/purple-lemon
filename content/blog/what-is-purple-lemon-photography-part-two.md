<h3>Wedding + Engagement Photography</h3>
<p>When Purple Lemon Photography was begun, it was out of a love for wedding photography that had developed as I worked as an associate for a wedding photography company in college. I discovered that I love wedding photography. It appeals to my love for creating and organizing events, celebrations, and relationships. The varied, long, fast-paced days are thrilling for me, and gives me much creative inspiration. I love working with the families and blessing the couple in every way I can.</p>
<p><img class="image__tall" src="https://res.cloudinary.com/purple-lemon-photography/image/upload/f_auto,fl_lossy.keep_attribution,q_auto/v1573268710/Posts/Files/bridal_shower_decoration_in_tower_grove_neighborhood__ihkoqj" alt="bridal shower decoration in tower grove neighborhood" width="664" height="1000" /></p>
<p><img class="image__tall" src="https://res.cloudinary.com/purple-lemon-photography/image/upload/f_auto,fl_lossy.keep_attribution,q_auto/v1573268711/Posts/Files/wedding_cake_photography_in_MO__h8cqbw" alt="wedding cake photography in MO" width="664" height="1000" /></p>
<p>Photographing engagements gives me time to build relationships with the couples I&rsquo;ll be spending the day with on their wedding day, and find ways to visually represent their story.</p>

<h3>Portraits</h3>

<p>Alongside weddings and engagements, I also photograph many babies, children, and families. I love using the skills I&rsquo;ve developed as a parent of children with sensory integration issues to engage children in fun and meaningful ways with their own families during their photography session.</p>
