<p>We want to show honor to God by valuing people, our resources, and the natural resources in the earth.&nbsp; Here is how we are doing that practically at PLP.</p>
<p>Using our equipment fully and until they die or show excess wear, rather than adding new gadgets and cameras and creating excess electronic waste and then passing the cost on to you</p>
<p>Reusing our packaging materials when we need to ship our orders</p>
<p>Recycled paper products and minimal new paper or plastics in our packaging choices</p>
<p>Recycled paper in many of our marketing materials</p>
<p><img src="https://res.cloudinary.com/purple-lemon-photography/image/upload/f_auto,fl_keep_attribution.lossy,q_auto/v1541295004/Posts/Files/IMG_20180524_110825292_HDR_636750818423042933__hsa6pl" alt="Purple Lemon business cards" class="image__tall" width="2398" height="3862" /></p>
<p>Paperless proofing</p>
<p>Paperless correspondence and billing</p>
<p>Keeping our staff level low so we can pay well</p>
<p>Consideration of our ethics in our purchases</p>
<p>Posting images of our clients and children online only with permission, and keeping children&rsquo;s names private</p>
<p>Meeting online with our out of town clients, rather than driving</p>
<p>Using real plates, napkins, and cups rather than disposable</p>
<p>Providing handmade, local treats and snacks when possible&nbsp;</p>
