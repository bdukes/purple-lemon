<p>I often tell people that I&rsquo;ll photograph year round, but rarely get takers in the winter. It&rsquo;s been fun over the last few years that families I&rsquo;ve photographed many times have realized that I&rsquo;ve photographed them in many nature-driven-seasons, so they reach out to make a complete set. Spring, summer, autumn, and winter.</p>
<p>Maybe it&rsquo;s the sunscreen I&rsquo;m smelling on my skin and the heat from the start of summer, but I&rsquo;m thankful for all the chances to photograph year round. I have been able to photograph under spring blossoms, summer&rsquo;s carnivals, in the crunch of fall leaves, and folks wrapped in blankets and sipping warm drinks and embracing for warmth.</p>
<p>I remember the hot sun glaring down on us during this family&rsquo;s engagement session and the cold air on their New Year&rsquo;s Eve wedding. And countless times with the additions of their sons in between. They reached out midwinter for snow photos to help round out their collection, and we hung in there, watching the weather and touching base every time there was a reasonable chance for snow.</p>
<p>It was so worth the wait and spur of the moment scheduling.</p>
